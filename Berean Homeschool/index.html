About Us

Berean Homeschool (BHS) is the fully-accredited homeschool program of BCI Marikina that seeks to equip #calledtohomeschool families to be intentionally engaging the learner's head, heart, and hands to the glory of God.

Program Overview

Grade Levels: Nursery - Grade 10

Learning Delivery: Parent-Led (All Subjects)

Learning Materials: Online materials, including an AI-enabled platform

Training: Orientation & Head, Heart, Hands Training

Assessments: Year-End Achievement Tests, Quarterly Bloom Reviews, Quarterly Online Exams, Socio-Emotional Tests

Community Engagement: Family Assembly, Family Day, Thanksgiving Celebration, Creative Arts & Athletics Department (CAAD) Classes, CSR Initiatives, BHS Huddles for Families, Account x Ability (AxA) Sessions for Students and Parents

Counselling: Berean High, Counselling Service
