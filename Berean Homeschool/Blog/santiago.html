A Gratitude Post for the Lord

Noli and Dra. Donna with Abi (Kinder) | Homeschooling Since 2023

Praising God and sharing our first year of homeschooling with our little one. As husband and wife, we both went to a traditional school. Studied through the guidance of our teachers. Went through life one day at a time together with our classmates and friends. Hence, homeschool is a really different turf for us.

But as we were exposed to the importance of influencing your children and cultivating a Godly legacy, we took the heed to start homeschooling through Berean Homeschool under the supervision of Ms. Peej Caguin Isiderio and still with guidance and help of our tutor Teacher Belle Garsain Dela Paz.

We have seen our little one from literally a crying baby (which breaks my heart) to a not so little one, now conquering not only her fears of being left in school with strangers but to conquer the stage with a lot of strangers looking at her. My husband and I are glad that our child continues to improve one day at a time, not only in her intellect but most specially in her emotional and spiritual health, which we believe is utmost important.

We are blessed with a family who supports our endeavors. A community that caters our concerns and differences. Most especially, thank You Lord for this journey.

We are still in Pre-School, malayo na pero malapit na rin. But still, we are excited to where God will teach us as a family.
